What is different about plumbing in Mounds

Mounds does not run on Sapulpa's systems. The town has its own Board of Trustees, its own school district and its own sewer, run by the Mounds Public Works Authority. Water comes through Creek County Rural Water District No. 7, the partner the Town of Mounds names for water service. So an outage or billing question goes to the district, not to a plumber.

The sewer uses a lagoon. In 2025 the Oklahoma Water Resources Board granted the authority $99,900 to add riprap to an eroding lagoon bank and replace a deteriorating manhole. When the water is off, and when it is your pipe

Rural water districts can lose pressure when a pump fails. On June 30, 2026, news outlets reported that both pumps at Creek County Rural Water District No. 7 failed and customers were asked to ration water. No plumber can fix that, but it changes what you check first.

Ask a neighbor whether their taps are dry too. If so, call the district at 918-827-6575 and wait. If the neighbors have water and you do not, the problem is on your property: a closed valve, a frozen line or a break. That is when you call us. A plumber can find the break with leak detection and fix a damaged supply run with water line repair.

After service returns, air and loosened sediment come through the pipes. Run a cold tap at the lowest point in the house until the water clears. Skip hot water and the dishwasher until then, so grit stays out of the water heater.

Before we arrive: what to do in the first ten minutes

A few quick steps limit damage while the plumber is on the way. Pick the card that matches your situation.

Leak inside the house

Turn the main shutoff valve clockwise until it stops. Open the lowest faucet to drain the lines, then move rugs and boxes away from the wet spot.

Water off on the whole street

Check with a neighbor, then call the district at 918-827-6575. Close your main valve so returning pressure cannot flood an open tap. Call us if only your house is dry.

Water near outlets or the panel

Do not stand in water to reach the breaker. If the panel is dry, switch off the circuits for the wet room. If not, leave and call 911.

Water heater leaking or sewage backing up

Shut the cold-water valve on a leaking tank and turn its gas control or breaker off. For a backup, stop running water and keep children and pets out.

If you smell gas, leave the house at once, avoid switches and flames, and call 911 first. Never wait for a plumber with gas in the house.
