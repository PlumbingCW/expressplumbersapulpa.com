What to have ready when you call

  • Your address and the nearest cross street, which helps a dispatcher place Sapulpa, Kellyville, Mounds and Glenpool addresses quickly.
  • What you can see or hear: water on a floor, a wet ceiling, a hissing wall, a backed-up toilet, a pilot light that will not stay lit.
  • Where your main water shutoff is, if you know. If you do not, the dispatcher will help you find it.
  • Whether you own or rent. Renters should also tell the landlord or property manager about the problem.
  • Anything you already tried, so the plumber does not repeat it.
