Privacy Policy

A short, plain description of what happens to information when you call or visit this site.

Call (855) 575-2890

This policy covers the website at expressplumbersapulpa.com and calls to the Sapulpa Express Plumbing line at (855) 575-2890.

Calls to our line

This site works by phone. When you call, we ask for your name, address and phone number, and for details about the plumbing problem, so a plumber can come out and help. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

We use call information to arrange service, to answer follow-up questions and to improve how the line works. We do not sell call recordings.

This website

The website has no forms, accounts or checkout. We do not ask you to type personal details into the site. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and may display a Google Maps embed, so Google may receive technical information such as your IP address when those parts load. See Google's own privacy policy for how it handles that information.

Want your real number? Call the line and describe what you see.

A dispatcher is on the line 24/7. Nobody here will pressure you into a repair, and the price is agreed before work starts.

Call (855) 575-2890

Email

If you email contact@expressplumbersapulpa.com we receive your address and the message you send. We use it only to reply.

Your choices

You can ask us not to contact you again, and you can ask what we hold about your calls, by phoning the line or emailing the address above. Plumbers and contractors who visit your home may keep their own records, and their handling of those records is covered by their own policies.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version always lives on this page.
